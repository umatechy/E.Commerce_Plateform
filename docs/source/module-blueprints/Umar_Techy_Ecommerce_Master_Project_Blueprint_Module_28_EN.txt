1. MODULE PURPOSE
------------------------------------------------------------

Module 28 defines the complete Affiliate, Referral, Reseller,
Partner, Commission, Attribution, Payout, and Reseller Store
Provisioning framework for the Umar Techy E-Commerce Platform.

The module must support:

- Umar Techy platform-level affiliate programs.
- Store/tenant-level affiliate programs.
- Reseller programs for selling Umar Techy packages/services.
- Referral links, referral codes, coupons, and campaigns.
- Customer, order, and store attribution.
- Commission calculation and lifecycle management.
- Commission approval, holding, reversal, and payout.
- Affiliate and reseller dashboards.
- Reseller-driven store provisioning.
- Custom reseller pricing and commercial rules.
- Tiered commission structures.
- Future sub-reseller capabilities.
- Fraud and abuse prevention.
- Tax/KYC/compliance foundations.
- Full auditability and reconciliation.
- Strict multi-tenant isolation.
- Integration with Orders, Customers, Promotions, Marketing,
  Billing, Analytics, Subscriptions, Payments, and Super Admin.

This module must not bypass the authoritative business rules of
other Core modules.

Affiliate and reseller functionality is a commercial orchestration
layer. Orders, payments, inventory, subscriptions, customer
identity, and tenant isolation remain authoritative in their
respective Core modules.


2. CORE ARCHITECTURAL PRINCIPLES
------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 One Core Program Engine

Affiliate and reseller functionality must be implemented through
one reusable Core Program Engine.

Do not create separate codebases for:

- Platform affiliates.
- Store affiliates.
- Resellers.
- Partners.
- Referral programs.

Program configuration and role-specific capabilities must
differentiate behavior.

2.2 Multi-Tenant by Design

Every tenant-owned affiliate program, campaign, referral code,
commission, customer attribution, order attribution, and report
must be tenant-scoped.

Platform-level programs may operate across multiple tenants only
through explicitly authorized platform rules.

Tenant context must be resolved before accessing tenant-owned
affiliate data.

2.3 Server Authority

The server is authoritative for:

- Attribution.
- Referral validity.
- Commission eligibility.
- Commission amount.
- Qualification rules.
- Order qualification.
- Subscription qualification.
- Refund/return impact.
- Chargeback impact.
- Payout eligibility.
- Reseller pricing.
- Reseller discount limits.
- Store provisioning permissions.

Client-provided attribution or commission amounts must never be
trusted.

2.4 Immutable Financial Snapshots

Commission calculations must preserve historical snapshots of:

- Program.
- Commission rule.
- Rate.
- Fixed amount.
- Order value.
- Eligible amount.
- Currency.
- Attribution source.
- Affiliate/reseller.
- Customer.
- Order/subscription/store.
- Qualification status.

Later changes to a program must not rewrite historical financial
records.

2.5 Separation of Attribution and Commission

Attribution determines who receives credit.

Commission rules determine how much credit is financially
recognized.

These concerns must remain separately modeled.

2.6 Separation of Commission and Payout

A commission can be approved without being immediately payable.

Commission records and payout records must remain separate.

2.7 No Direct Financial Mutation

The module must never directly modify payment provider balances,
order totals, subscription invoices, or financial ledger authority
owned by other modules.

It may create commission obligations and payout instructions through
approved interfaces.


3. PROGRAM TYPES
------------------------------------------------------------

The system must support the following program types.

3.1 PLATFORM_AFFILIATE

Managed by Umar Techy.

Purpose:

- Refer customers to the Umar Techy platform.
- Refer new stores.
- Refer subscriptions/packages.
- Refer hosting/domain/services where applicable.
- Earn platform-defined commissions.

3.2 STORE_AFFILIATE

Owned by an individual tenant/store.

Purpose:

- Allow a store to recruit affiliates.
- Affiliates promote store products.
- Commission is calculated from qualifying store orders.

3.3 PLATFORM_RESELLER

Managed by Umar Techy.

Purpose:

- Resellers sell Umar Techy packages/services.
- Resellers may receive special pricing.
- Resellers may receive recurring or one-time commissions.
- Resellers may provision or onboard stores if authorized.

3.4 STORE_RESELLER

Optional future program.

A store may authorize a partner to sell its products or services
under controlled reseller rules.

3.5 PARTNER_PROGRAM

Generalized future partner model supporting agencies,
consultants, implementation partners, technology partners, or
service partners.

3.6 REFERRAL_PROGRAM

Customer referral program that may reward:

- Referrer.
- Referred customer.
- Both.

Rewards may be monetary, coupon-based, store credit, loyalty
points, or other configured rewards.

23. RETURNS, REFUNDS AND REVERSALS
------------------------------------------------------------

- Full commission reversal.

Partial refund:
- Proportional or rule-based commission reversal.

Returned product:
- Reverse commission for returned eligible item.

Cancelled order:
- Remove or reverse commission depending on lifecycle state.

Chargeback:
- Mark affected commission for reversal/recovery.

Historical commission records must remain auditable.

------------------------------------------------------------

1. One Core Affiliate & Reseller Engine.
2. Multi-tenant isolation is mandatory.
3. Platform programs and tenant programs are distinct scopes.
4. Affiliate identity is separate from customer identity.
5. Reseller identity is separate from store ownership.
6. Attribution and commission are separate concerns.
7. Commission and payout are separate records.
8. Server is authoritative for all financial calculations.
9. Client-provided commission amounts are never trusted.
10. Client-provided attribution cannot override server rules.
11. Commission calculations must be reproducible.
12. Commission snapshots are immutable.
13. Published program versions are immutable.
14. Refunds and returns can reverse commissions.
15. COD commissions may require delivery/collection qualification.
16. Subscription commissions require explicit rules.
17. Payouts require idempotency.
18. Payout reconciliation is mandatory.
19. Duplicate payouts must be impossible through normal retries.
20. Referral links must never become open redirects.
21. Referral codes must be unique within scope.
22. Self-referral protection is mandatory.
23. Fraud controls must be configurable.
24. Fraud signals are not automatically proof unless policy defines it.
25. Reseller pricing is server-controlled.
26. Store provisioning must be idempotent.
27. Reseller access never equals Super Admin access.
28. Tenant context must be resolved before tenant data access.
29. Cache keys must preserve tenant/program identity.
30. Analytics is not the financial source of truth.
31. Orders remain authoritative for order state.
32. Payments remain authoritative for payment state.
33. Subscriptions remain authoritative for subscription state.
34. Billing remains authoritative for invoices and renewals.
35. Promotions remain authoritative for discount rules.
36. Inventory remains authoritative for stock.
37. Shipping remains authoritative for delivery state.
38. Notifications remain authoritative for message delivery.
39. Backup/restore must not duplicate financial effects.
40. Financial records must remain auditable.
41. Sensitive payout/compliance data must be protected.
42. Marketing consent must not be bypassed.
43. Affiliate tracking must minimize personal data.
44. External integrations must use approved APIs.
45. Third-party extensions cannot bypass Core authority.
46. Background jobs must be idempotent.
47. Webhooks must be authenticated and replay-protected.
48. Queue failures must not lose qualifying financial events.
49. Checkout must not depend on affiliate analytics availability.
50. Affiliate tracking must not materially degrade checkout.

53. Affiliate suspension must be auditable.

55. Historical commission obligations must follow program terms.
