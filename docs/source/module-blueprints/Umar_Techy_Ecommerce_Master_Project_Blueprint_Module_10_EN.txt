MODULE 10 — CUSTOMER MANAGEMENT
Version: 1.0
Status: Planning / Blueprint

============================================================
1. MODULE PURPOSE
============================================================

This module defines the centralized Customer Management
architecture for the Umar Techy multi-tenant E-Commerce Platform.

The customer system manages the customer identity and
store-specific relationship required for:

- Customer Accounts
- Guest Customers
- Profiles
- Contact Information
- Addresses
- Order History
- Customer Groups
- Customer Segmentation
- Customer Notes
- Customer Tags
- Preferences
- Communication Preferences
- Marketing Consent
- Customer Status
- Customer History
- Loyalty foundation
- Customer analytics foundation
- Privacy controls
- Account security
- Import / Export
- API
- Integrations
- Multi-store identity readiness

The system must preserve a strict distinction between platform
identity and store-level customer data.

============================================================
2. CORE CUSTOMER PRINCIPLE
============================================================

ONE CORE CUSTOMER ENGINE
+
PLATFORM IDENTITY WHERE NEEDED
+
STORE-SCOPED CUSTOMER RELATIONSHIP
+
STRICT TENANT ISOLATION
+
PRIVACY BY DESIGN

must serve all stores.

Basic, Business, and Premium stores use the same Customer Engine.

Package differences are enforced through Module 04.

============================================================
3. CUSTOMER VS USER
============================================================

A CUSTOMER is a shopper who purchases from a store.

A USER is an authenticated platform/store administration
identity defined in Module 02.

Customers must not automatically receive administrative
permissions.

Customer authentication and staff authentication should remain
logically separated even if they share infrastructure.

============================================================
4. PLATFORM IDENTITY VS STORE CUSTOMER
============================================================

The architecture should support a future model where one human
may interact with multiple stores.

Conceptually:

Platform Identity
    ↓
Store Customer A
Store Customer B
Store Customer C

However, Store A must never receive Store B's private customer
data without explicit and authorized platform-level functionality.

The first release may use store-scoped customer accounts while
remaining architecture-ready for broader identity federation.

============================================================
5. TENANT ISOLATION
============================================================

Every store customer relationship must be tenant-scoped.

Store A must never access:

- Store B customers
- Store B addresses
- Store B orders through customer APIs
- Store B customer notes
- Store B tags
- Store B segments
- Store B marketing consent
- Store B loyalty balance
- Store B private customer metadata

All APIs, queries, caches, exports, events, jobs, analytics, and
integrations must enforce tenant scope.

8. GUEST CUSTOMER
============================================================

A guest customer may place an order without creating an account.

The system may create a temporary or store-scoped customer
record associated with the order.

Guest data should be retained only as required for:

- Order processing
- Customer communication
- Returns
- Refunds
- Business records
- Legal requirements

============================================================
9. GUEST TO REGISTERED CONVERSION
============================================================

A guest customer may later create an account.

The conversion process must safely associate eligible historical
orders with the new customer identity.

The system must avoid accidentally linking another person's
orders based only on weak matching such as name.

Verification should be required.

100. FINAL ARCHITECTURAL RULES
============================================================

1. One Core Customer Engine.
2. Customer data must be tenant-scoped.
3. Customer and staff identity must remain logically distinct.
4. Guest customers must be supported.
5. Guest-to-account conversion must require secure verification.
6. Customer profiles are mutable.
7. Order customer snapshots are historical and immutable.
8. Customer addresses must be customer-owned and tenant-scoped.
9. Address IDs alone must never authorize access.
10. Customer groups must not grant administrative permissions.
11. Customer segmentation must be tenant-scoped.
12. Marketing consent must be explicitly tracked.
13. Consent history must be auditable.
14. Transactional and marketing communication must be distinct.
15. Customer private data must not be publicly cached.
16. Customer search indexes must be tenant-aware.
17. Customer exports must be permission-controlled and audited.
18. Customer imports must validate before committing.
19. Sensitive customer changes require appropriate verification.
20. Customer merge must be explicit and tenant-safe.
21. Historical orders must survive customer changes.
22. Deletion must respect business/legal retention requirements.
23. Privacy workflows must support controlled anonymization.
24. External integrations must use stable external references.
25. Customer architecture must remain extensible for loyalty,
    CRM, AI, POS, marketplace, and future identity features.
