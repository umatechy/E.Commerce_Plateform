============================================================
3. DISCOUNT VS COUPON VS PROMOTION
============================================================

DISCOUNT:
A reduction in price.

COUPON:
A customer-entered or system-applied code that activates an
eligible offer.

PROMOTION:
A commercial rule or campaign that determines eligibility and
discount behavior.

A promotion may exist without a coupon.

A coupon may reference a promotion.

5. PERCENTAGE DISCOUNT
============================================================

Example:

10% off

The system must calculate the discount from the eligible
amount only.

============================================================
6. FIXED AMOUNT DISCOUNT
============================================================

Example:

PKR 500 off

The system must prevent the discount from reducing the eligible
amount below the configured minimum/zero unless negative totals
are explicitly supported, which is not recommended.

============================================================
7. PRODUCT-LEVEL DISCOUNT
============================================================

Promotion may target:

- Product
- Variant
- SKU

Only eligible items receive the discount.

============================================================
8. CATEGORY-LEVEL DISCOUNT
============================================================

Promotion may target one or more categories.

Example:

20% off all products in "Shoes"

Category eligibility must respect the current tenant's taxonomy.

============================================================
9. COLLECTION DISCOUNT
============================================================

Promotions may target store collections.

Example:

Summer Collection → 15% off

============================================================
10. BRAND DISCOUNT
============================================================

Promotion may target selected brands.

Example:

Brand A → 10% off

============================================================
11. ORDER-LEVEL DISCOUNT
============================================================

Discount applies to the eligible order subtotal.

Example:

PKR 1,000 off orders above PKR 10,000.

============================================================
12. QUANTITY DISCOUNT
============================================================

Examples:

Buy 2 → 5% off
Buy 5 → 10% off

Quantity calculation must use validated quantities.

102. FINAL ARCHITECTURAL RULES
============================================================

1. One Core Promotion Engine.
2. All promotions and coupons are tenant-scoped.
3. Final discounts are calculated server-side.
4. Client-submitted discount values must never be trusted.
5. Coupon codes are unique within the appropriate store scope.
6. Promotion rules must be deterministic.
7. Promotion priority must be deterministic.
8. Stacking behavior must be explicit.
9. Exclusions must be explicitly evaluated.
10. Usage limits must be concurrency-safe.
11. Abandoned carts must not permanently consume coupon usage
    unless configured.
12. Promotion application must be auditable.
13. Orders must store immutable promotion snapshots.
14. Later promotion edits must not rewrite historical orders.
15. Product, category, brand, collection, customer, payment, and
    shipping targeting must remain extensible.
16. Promotion and payment state must remain separate.
