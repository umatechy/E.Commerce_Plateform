MODULE 13 — SHIPPING & DELIVERY MANAGEMENT
Version: 1.0
Status: Planning / Blueprint

============================================================
1. MODULE PURPOSE
============================================================

This module defines the Core Shipping and Delivery Management
architecture for the Umar Techy multi-tenant E-Commerce Platform.

The shipping system must allow every store to configure and
operate its own delivery rules while sharing one core shipping
engine.

The module manages:

- Shipping Zones
- Countries
- Provinces / States
- Cities
- Areas / Postal Codes
- Shipping Methods
- Delivery Rates
- Free Shipping
- Flat Rate Shipping
- Weight-Based Shipping
- Price-Based Shipping
- Quantity-Based Shipping
- Local Delivery
- Store Pickup
- Courier Integration
- Shipment Creation
- Tracking
- Delivery Status
- Estimated Delivery
- Shipping Restrictions
- COD Serviceability
- Multiple Warehouses
- Split Fulfillment Foundation
- Returns Shipping Foundation
- Shipping Labels Foundation
- Shipping Analytics
- Shipping Events
- Shipping Webhooks
- Tenant Isolation
- Future Carrier Expansion

============================================================
2. CORE ARCHITECTURAL PRINCIPLE
============================================================

ONE CORE SHIPPING ENGINE
+
STORE-SPECIFIC SHIPPING CONFIGURATION
+
MULTIPLE SHIPPING METHODS
+
MULTIPLE CARRIERS
+
MULTIPLE WAREHOUSES
+
TENANT ISOLATION
+
SERVER-AUTHORITATIVE SHIPPING CALCULATION

must serve all stores.

Basic, Business, and Premium packages use the same engine.

Package differences are enforced through Module 04.

============================================================
3. SHIPPING VS DELIVERY
============================================================

Shipping refers to the commercial and operational process of
moving an order from store/warehouse to customer.

Delivery refers to the final movement and completion of the
shipment.

The architecture must keep:

- Shipping Method
- Shipment
- Carrier
- Tracking
- Delivery Status

as related but separate concepts.

============================================================
4. SHIPPING SCOPE
============================================================

Shipping configuration is store-scoped.

Store A may use:

- Courier A
- Flat Rate
- Free Shipping

Store B may use:

- Courier B
- Local Delivery
- Store Pickup

No store may access another store's shipping configuration,
carrier credentials, rates, shipments, or tracking data.

============================================================
18. WEIGHT-BASED SHIPPING
============================================================

Example:

0–1 kg = PKR 200
1–3 kg = PKR 300
3–5 kg = PKR 450

The engine must calculate total shipment weight from authoritative
product/variant data.

Client-submitted weight must never be trusted.

============================================================
19. PRICE-BASED SHIPPING
============================================================

Example:

Order < PKR 3,000 → PKR 250
Order ≥ PKR 3,000 → PKR 0

Rules must be configurable.

============================================================
20. QUANTITY-BASED SHIPPING
============================================================

Example:

1–2 Items = PKR 200
3–5 Items = PKR 350

Quantity must be calculated from validated cart items.

============================================================
21. PRODUCT-SPECIFIC SHIPPING
============================================================

Products may require special shipping rules.

Examples:

- Oversized Product
- Fragile Product

- Refrigerated Product
- Digital Product
- No-Shipping Product

============================================================
22. CATEGORY SHIPPING RULES
============================================================

Future rules may apply to categories.

Possible strategies:

- Highest Rate

- Carrier Calculation

============================================================

============================================================
108. FINAL ARCHITECTURAL RULES
============================================================

1. One Core Shipping Engine.
2. Shipping configuration is tenant-scoped.
3. Shipping rates must be calculated server-side.
4. Client-submitted shipping prices must never be trusted.
5. Shipping serviceability must be server-authoritative.
6. Shipping rules must have deterministic priority.
7. Product, shipping class, zone, and carrier rules must be
   composable.
8. Digital-only carts must not require physical shipping.
9. Store pickup must be a first-class shipping/fulfillment method.
10. Local delivery must use the same core shipping architecture.
11. Carrier integrations must use provider/adapter abstraction.
12. Carrier credentials must remain tenant-scoped and secure.
13. Carrier webhooks must be verified and idempotent.
14. Tracking history must be preserved.
15. Shipment and Order are separate but integrated entities.
16. One Order may contain multiple Shipments.
17. Split shipments must be supported by the architecture.
18. Inventory remains authoritative in Module 08.
19. Historical order/shipping address snapshots must not change
    when customer addresses change.
20. Shipping quotes may expire and must be revalidated before
    final Order creation.
21. Actual carrier cost and customer shipping charge must remain
    separately traceable.
22. Free shipping does not mean carrier cost is zero.
23. Shipping refunds/return shipping must integrate with Module 09.
24. Shipment failures must not destroy historical shipment data.
25. Duplicate shipment creation must be prevented with idempotency.
26. Payment and shipping state must remain separate.
27. Package entitlements may control shipping features and limits.
28. Shipping architecture must remain ready for multiple
    warehouses, multiple carriers, international delivery,
    automated routing, pickup, returns, and future logistics APIs.
29. Security, privacy, tenant isolation, auditability, and
    failure recovery are mandatory.
