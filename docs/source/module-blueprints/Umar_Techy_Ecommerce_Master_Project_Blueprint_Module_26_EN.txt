1. MODULE PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Module 26 provides the Core AI Features, AI Orchestration, AI
Assistants, Recommendations, Search Intelligence, Content
Generation, Store Intelligence, Automation, AI Usage Metering, AI
Safety, Provider Abstraction, and future AI Agent foundation for the
Umar Techy Multi-Tenant E-Commerce Platform.

AI must enhance commerce workflows without becoming the authority for
financial, inventory, security, identity, payment, shipping, or
tenant-isolation decisions.

Core principle:

AI ASSISTS
        +
DETERMINISTIC BUSINESS RULES
        +
SERVER-AUTHORITATIVE DATA
        +
TENANT ISOLATION
        +
HUMAN CONTROL
        +
SAFETY
        +
AUDITABILITY

4. AI AUTHORITY PRINCIPLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
AI is advisory or assistive by default.

AI must not independently become the source of truth for:

- Price.
- Tax.
- Inventory quantity.
- Payment amount.
- Refund amount.
- Shipping rate.
- Order status.
- Subscription status.
- Permissions.
- Identity.
- Tenant isolation.


5. DETERMINISTIC BUSINESS RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
When AI output conflicts with a deterministic business rule:

DETERMINISTIC BUSINESS RULE WINS.

Examples:

AI suggests an unavailable product:
→ Inventory authority decides availability.

AI suggests a discount:
→ Promotion Engine validates the discount.

AI suggests a refund:
→ Order/Payment systems validate the refund.

AI suggests a shipping option:
→ Shipping Engine validates serviceability/rate.


6. TENANT ISOLATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------
AI requests must preserve:

- Tenant ID.
- User/customer authorization.
- Store context.
- Data scope.
- Feature entitlement.

AI context must never include another tenant's data.

Cross-tenant AI context leakage is a critical security failure.

10. SANDBOX / PRODUCTION SEPARATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------
AI development/testing environments must not use production side
effects.

Test AI calls must not:

- Send real marketing messages.
- Create real orders.
- Trigger real payments.
- Change live inventory.
- Modify production configuration.

Use mocked or isolated tools.
