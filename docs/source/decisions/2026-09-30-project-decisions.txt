PROJECT DECISIONS — CLARIFICATION BASELINE

1. TAX POLICY
Tax must be configurable per store and jurisdiction.
The platform must support tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive pricing,
tax classes, configurable tax rates, and applicable regions.
No tax rate may be hardcoded into the core platform.
For the initial Pakistan launch, actual tax rules/rates will be
configured only after applicable current legal/tax requirements are
confirmed.

2. PAYMENT / SHIPPING PROVIDERS
Initial payment gateway architecture shall support:
- JazzCash
- Easypaisa
- Card payments through a suitable card gateway/provider

Use a provider adapter architecture.

No real production credentials exist yet and no fake credentials may be
created. Sandbox/test credentials must be supplied separately by the
providers and stored securely outside Git.

Shipping/courier must also use a provider adapter. The first Pakistan
courier provider will be selected during implementation based on actual
API and sandbox availability. Do not hardcode a courier provider into the
core architecture before this verification.

3. SMS / WHATSAPP
Initial WhatsApp provider:
- Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API

SMS:
- Provider abstraction from day one.
- Initial provider may be Twilio or a suitable Pakistan-local SMS
  provider after API, sender-ID, delivery and pricing verification.

The Notification domain must remain provider-agnostic.

4. CRITICAL ALERTS
Initial critical operational alert channels:
- Email: mandatory
- WhatsApp: configurable for critical alerts
- Slack: future-ready, not required for initial implementation

Critical alerts may include:
- backup failure
- restore failure
- severe store-health problems
- infrastructure failure
- important security events
- other explicitly configured critical conditions

5. RETENTION / RPO / RTO
Closed/suspended store retention baseline:
- 90 days after permanent closure/suspension, unless legal/contractual
  requirements require longer retention.

Backup retention baseline:
- Daily backups: 30 days
- Monthly backups: 12 months
- Additional pre-change/critical backups according to backup policy

Initial recovery targets:
- RPO: 24 hours
- RTO: 4 hours

These are TARGETS, not claims of achieved production capability.
Actual RPO/RTO must be verified through real backup/restore testing.

6. INVENTORY COSTING
Initial inventory module does NOT implement accounting valuation.

Do not implement:
- FIFO
- Weighted Average Cost
- another accounting costing method

The initial inventory system maintains stock quantities, movements,
reservations and the stock ledger.

Future FIFO or Weighted Average Cost may be added through a separately
approved accounting/costing requirement.

7. AI PROVIDER
Initial AI provider:
- OpenAI API

However, Module 26 must use a provider abstraction so other providers
can be added later.

AI credentials must remain in secure environment/configuration and never
be committed to Git.

Before implementing individual AI features, Module 26 must define:
- feature list
- provider/model selection
- data/privacy rules
- entitlement requirements
- rate limits
- usage/cost controls
- audit requirements
- failure/fallback behavior

Do not invent production credentials, live provider access, sandbox
credentials, tax rates, or provider capabilities.
