1. DOCUMENT PURPOSE
====================================================================

This SRS defines the functional, non-functional, security, data, API,
integration, operational and quality requirements of the Umar Techy
E-Commerce Platform.

It converts the product vision and Project Bible into implementable system
requirements.

This document is authoritative for system behavior. Detailed module
implementation must follow the corresponding Module Blueprint while
remaining consistent with this SRS and the Project Bible.

====================================================================
2. SYSTEM SCOPE
====================================================================

The system is a multi-tenant SaaS platform through which independent
businesses can create, configure, operate and grow e-commerce stores.

The platform shall provide:

- Account and identity management
- Multi-tenant store management
- Roles and permissions
- Packages and entitlements
- Storefronts
- Catalog
- Categories, brands and attributes
- Inventory
- Orders
- Customers
- Cart, wishlist and checkout
- Payments
- Shipping
- Promotions
- Marketing
- SEO and content
- Themes and branding
- Animation
- Domains
- Hosting/infrastructure controls
- Notifications
- Analytics and reporting
- Backup and restore
- Store health monitoring
- PWA/mobile experience
- AI services
- Marketplace and integrations
- Affiliate/reseller functionality
- Billing/subscriptions
- Super Admin
- Developer APIs
- Security/audit/compliance
- System configuration
- Help/support/documentation
- Future extension framework

3. SYSTEM ACTORS
====================================================================

3.1 Platform Super Admin
Manages platform-wide configuration, tenants, packages, security,
integrations, billing, monitoring, support and governance.

3.2 Platform Operations/Security Staff
Performs explicitly authorized operational and security tasks.

3.3 Store Owner
Owns or administers a store and controls permitted business operations.

3.4 Store Administrator/Manager
Manages permitted store operations according to assigned permissions.

3.5 Store Staff
Performs assigned operational tasks such as orders, catalog or inventory.

3.6 Marketing User
Manages marketing, promotions, content and permitted analytics.

3.7 Finance/Accounts User
Manages permitted billing, invoices and financial workflows.

3.8 Customer
Browses stores, creates accounts, manages cart, checks out and manages
their own permitted customer data and orders.

3.9 Developer/Integration Partner
Uses authorized APIs, webhooks, SDKs and integrations.

3.10 Affiliate/Reseller
Uses authorized referral, attribution and reseller capabilities.

4. MULTI-TENANT REQUIREMENTS
====================================================================

MT-001 The system shall support multiple independent tenants.

MT-002 Every tenant shall have a stable unique Tenant ID.

MT-003 Every store shall have a stable Store ID.

MT-004 Tenant ownership and store ownership shall be explicit.

MT-005 Tenant-owned records shall be associated with the correct tenant.

MT-006 API requests shall resolve and validate tenant context.

MT-007 Background jobs shall carry validated tenant context.

MT-008 Cache keys shall prevent cross-tenant collisions and leakage.

MT-009 Search indexes shall enforce tenant isolation.

MT-010 File storage paths and access policies shall be tenant-aware.

MT-011 Exports shall contain only authorized tenant data.

MT-012 Analytics shall respect tenant authorization.

MT-013 Webhooks shall resolve the correct tenant context.

MT-014 Cross-tenant access shall require explicit platform-level authority.

MT-015 Store upgrades/downgrades shall preserve Tenant ID, Store ID and
existing data unless an explicit documented retention policy applies.

MT-016 A tenant suspension shall not silently delete tenant data.

MT-017 Tenant deletion shall follow an explicit lifecycle and retention policy.

MT-018 Tenant isolation shall be tested as a dedicated security category.

51. REQUIREMENT PRIORITY MODEL
====================================================================

P0 — Mandatory for safe operation / security / core commerce.
P1 — Required for production commercial operation.
P2 — Important capability with controlled rollout.
P3 — Future/optional enhancement.

Security, tenant isolation, authorization, financial correctness, data
integrity and safe deployment are always treated as P0 regardless of package.

====================================================================
52. FINAL REQUIREMENT PRINCIPLE
====================================================================

The platform must be implemented as one coherent multi-tenant SaaS product.

No individual module may weaken:

- Tenant isolation
- Security
- Authorization
- Financial correctness
- Data integrity
- Auditability
- Maintainability
- Future extensibility

When a requirement is ambiguous, implementation must stop at the relevant
decision point, document the ambiguity and resolve it through the project's
governance process rather than silently inventing behavior.
