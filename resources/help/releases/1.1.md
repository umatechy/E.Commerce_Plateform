## New

- **A page for everything you manage**: products, customers, shipping, payments, promotions, pages, theme, domains, reports, backups, team and settings.
- **Your team**: invite staff by email and give each person a role. Make your own roles.
- **Urdu storefronts**: show your shop in Urdu, right to left, as well as English. Translate product, category and brand names.
- **Mobile wallets**: customers pay with JazzCash or Easypaisa and approve on their phone. The money goes to your own merchant account.
- **Cash on delivery and couriers**: limits for cash orders, shipments, tracking, and collecting cash from your courier.
- **WhatsApp and SMS order updates**, with your customers' consent.
- **Returns**: approve, receive, inspect, then refund, give store credit or send new items.
- **Store credit** for your customers.
- **Tax**: your own tax classes and rates. Nothing is charged until you turn it on.
- **Catalog tools**: collections, badges, attributes and filters, starter templates by business type, and import and export with spreadsheets.
- **Themes**: choose a theme, colours, fonts, layout, home page sections and motion. Preview before you publish.
- **Billing**: change your plan, report a bank transfer or pay online where offered, credit notes and PDFs.
- **Close or reopen your store**, and download your data at any time.
- **Help center**: search help from any page, page help with the Help button, and a help center for your shoppers.
- **Developer API**: a full API reference, webhook events you choose, and safe retries.

## Improved

- The dashboard shows a setup checklist with your progress and what is still required before launch.
- Dates follow your store's timezone everywhere.
- Prices are shown in Pakistani rupees (Rs.) by default.

## Security

- Two-step sign-in is required for store owners.
- Sensitive changes ask you to confirm it is you.
- See where you are signed in and sign out other devices.

## Known issues

- Card payments are not available yet: Umar Techy is choosing a card payment provider.
- Booking couriers straight from the admin needs a courier connected to the platform; until then, add tracking numbers yourself.
