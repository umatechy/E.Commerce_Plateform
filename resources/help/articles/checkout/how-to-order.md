1. Open a product, choose its options (such as size or colour) and press **Add to cart**.
2. Open your [cart](store:/cart) to check the items and quantities. Have a coupon code? Enter it at checkout.
3. Press **Checkout** and enter your name, email, phone and delivery address. The delivery options for your address and their cost are shown.
4. Choose how you want to pay. See [Ways to pay](help:ways-to-pay).
5. Press **Place order**.

You see your order number at once, and a confirmation is sent to your email.

## With or without an account

You can order as a guest. With an [account](store:/account/register) you can see all your orders in one place, save addresses and ask for returns more easily.

## Order updates on WhatsApp

If the store offers it, you can tick **Send my order updates on WhatsApp**. You only get news about this order, to the number you entered. Reply STOP to end them.

## If something stops you

- "We do not deliver to this address yet": the store does not deliver there. Try another address or [contact the store](store:/contact).
- A product is no longer available: it sold out while it was in your cart.
