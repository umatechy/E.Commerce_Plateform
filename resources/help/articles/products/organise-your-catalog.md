| Use | When you want to |
|---|---|
| [Categories](/categories) | Group products the way shoppers browse (Clothes → Shirts). A product has one main category. |
| [Brands](/brands) | Say who makes a product. A product carries one brand. |
| [Attributes](/attributes) | Describe products with properties such as size, colour or RAM. Each category chooses which attributes apply and which shoppers can filter by. |
| [Collections](/collections) | Make a group across categories, such as "Eid gifts". Hand-pick the products, or let rules choose them. Each collection has its own page and can fill a home page section. |
| [Badges](/badges) | Put a label on product cards, such as "New" or "Sale". Some badges are automatic; you can define your own. |

## Starting from a template

When your store was created you could start from a ready-made structure for your kind of business. You can also apply a starter template from [Categories](/categories). It only adds what is missing: nothing you made is changed or removed.

## Translations

Category, brand, collection and badge names can be translated into Urdu for an Urdu storefront.
