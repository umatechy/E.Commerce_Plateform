Open [Products](/products) and press **Add product**, or go straight to [Add product](/products/new).

## The essentials

- **Name** and a **short description** (up to 500 characters, shown in product lists).
- **Price** in your store currency. A **compare-at price**, if you use one, must be higher than the price: shoppers see the difference as a saving.
- **Cost**, if you want it for your own reports. It is never shown to customers.
- **Status**: only **active** products can be bought.
- **Main category**, so shoppers find it. Create categories first under [Categories](/categories).
- **Tax class**, if you charge tax. See [Set up tax](help:tax-setup).

## Pictures

Choose JPG or PNG files from your computer: up to 12 pictures, 5 MB each. The first one is the main picture.

## Variants

Variants are sellable versions of the product, such as sizes or colours. They are added **after** the product is created. Each variant has its own price and stock. Without variants, the product is sold as a single item at its own price.

## Merchandising

Mark a product as **featured** to show it in the "Featured products" section of your home page. Add tags (for example Eid, Handmade) and add it to hand-picked collections. See [Organise your catalog](help:organise-your-catalog).

## Stock

A product shows as in stock once its stock is tracked in a warehouse. See [Manage your stock](help:manage-stock).

## Package limits

Your package may limit how many products you can have. The products page shows how many you use. When you reach the limit, adding another is refused until you remove one or change your plan.
