Your storefront can be installed as an app: customers add it to their phone's home screen and open it like any other app. There is nothing to publish in an app store and nothing for customers to download. Open [Mobile app](/storefront/app).

## Before customers can install it

The page lists what is still needed:

- your package includes the app;
- the app is turned on;
- your shop is open to customers;
- an **app icon**: a square JPG or PNG of at least 512 × 512 pixels. Keep the important part in the middle, because some phones round the corners;
- a secure (https) address. Phones install apps only from secure addresses.

## Make it yours

- **App name** and the shorter **name under the icon** (up to 12 letters). Empty: your store name.
- **Top bar colour** and **opening screen colour**. Empty: the colours of your theme.
- **How it opens**: like an app without a browser bar, with small browser buttons, or in the browser.
- **App buttons at the bottom of phones**: Home, Shop, Search, Cart and Account, in the installed app only, on every phone, or never.
- **Suggest installing to returning visitors**: a small offer after a visitor has looked at a few pages. Once dismissed, it does not come back for a month. iPhones get a short note on how to add the shop from the Share menu.

The preview shows the icon on a home screen and the opening screen. Customers get changes the next time they open your shop.

## Without a connection

The app keeps the shop's public pages a customer has visited, so they can look at them without a connection. A notice says that these may be out of date. Cart and account pages are never kept on the phone. **Placing an order always needs a connection**, so prices, stock and delivery are checked by the server.

When you close your store, or turn the app off, the app on customers' phones clears what it kept and stops working offline.

## Usage

The page shows, for the last 30 days, how often the app was installed, opened from a home screen and used offline. Nothing that identifies a phone is collected. iPhones do not report installs.

Notifications on the phone are not available yet.
