Open [Theme](/storefront/theme). **You edit a draft**: customers see nothing until you publish.

## What you can change

- **Theme**: choosing one puts it in your draft with its own colours, fonts, layout and animation. Your logo, texts and home page sections stay.
- **Colours and type**: six-digit colours such as #1A2B3C, heading and body fonts, corner rounding. Leave a colour empty to use the theme's own.
- **Layout**: how the header, product lists and footer are arranged. Some layouts are in the Premium package.
- **Animation**: how sections ease in and cards move. Customers whose device asks for reduced motion always get a still storefront.
- **Logo, icon and texts**: choose a JPG or PNG (logo about 400 × 120 pixels, up to 5 MB) and a square icon for the browser tab. Your tagline can be written in each storefront language.
- **Social links**.
- **Home page sections**: banners, featured products, collections and more, in the order you choose.
- **Custom CSS**, if your package includes it. Scripts and imports are removed.

## Preview and publish

Use **Preview** to see the draft in a new tab. Press **Publish** when you are happy: customers see it at once.
