Your store always has its address on the Umar Techy platform. With a package that includes it, you can add your own domain on [Domains](/domains).

## Add and verify

1. Press **Add domain** and enter a domain you own, such as shop.example.com.
2. You are shown a **TXT record**. Copy it now: it is shown once, and it is valid for 7 days.
3. At the company where you manage your domain, add that record.
4. Come back and press **Check now**. DNS changes can take from minutes to a day to be visible.

When the record is found, the domain is verified. The page never marks a domain verified without finding the record.

To send visitors to your store, your domain must also point to Umar Techy's servers. Ask Umar Techy support from [Support](/support) for the details for your domain.

## Main address

Choose **Make main address** on an active domain to make it the address your store uses in links and search engines.

## SSL

The SSL column shows the certificate status of each domain as the platform reports it.
