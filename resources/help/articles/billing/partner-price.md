Some stores come to Umar Techy through a partner: an agency or consultant who sells Umar Techy. The partner either created your store and invited you to take it over, or sent you a sign-up link.

## The partner price

Your invoices show **partner price** when your store pays less than the public price because of your partner. The price applies to renewals and plan changes too. It is worked out by Umar Techy on every invoice; nobody can change it by hand.

## Your store is yours

- You are the store's owner. Your partner is not a member of your store and cannot sign in to it.
- Your partner sees your store's name, its package, whether it is in its trial or paying, and whether you accepted the invitation. They never see your customers, orders, payments or settings.
- If you want your team to include your partner, invite them under Store → Team like anyone else. You choose their role.

If you no longer work with your partner, contact Umar Techy support.
