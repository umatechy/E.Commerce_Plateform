Open [Plan and billing](/billing).

## Change plan

Choose **Change plan** and the package you want. Before anything changes you see what it costs and what changes:

- **Moving up** happens now. You pay the difference for the rest of the period you already paid for.
- **Moving down** happens at the end of the period you have paid for; until then you keep your current package.
- **During a trial**, you move now and the trial continues; nothing is charged now.

If the new package allows fewer of something than you have (for example products), nothing is deleted: you cannot add more until you are under the limit.

## Monthly or yearly

Switching the billing interval applies from your next billing period.

## Cancel

Choose to cancel your subscription and tell us why. Your store keeps working until the end of the period you have paid for; after that it closes to customers. You can change your mind until then.

To close the store at once and take your data with you, see [Close your store](help:close-store).
