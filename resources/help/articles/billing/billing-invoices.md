[Plan and billing](/billing) shows your package, what it includes and uses, and your invoices from Umar Techy. Billing dates are UTC dates. Each invoice can be downloaded as a PDF.

## Pay an invoice

- **Bank transfer or wallet**: pay to Umar Techy's account shown on the invoice. Then open the invoice and choose **I have paid**: enter the amount and the transaction or reference number. The invoice counts as paid once the Umar Techy team has checked your payment. Your reports are listed under **Payments you reported**.
- **Pay online (JazzCash / Easypaisa)**, where it is offered. If online payment is not available yet, pay by transfer and tell us with "I have paid".

## Credit

- **Account credit** from Umar Techy pays towards your next invoices first.
- A **credit note** reduces what you owe for an invoice, for example after a correction. Credit notes are listed with your invoices.

## Your first invoice

When Umar Techy asks for payment before launch, the setup checklist on your dashboard has **Get my first invoice**. See [Set up your store and launch it](help:setup-checklist).
