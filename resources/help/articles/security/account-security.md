Open [Security](/security) from the menu under your name.

## Password

Use at least 10 characters, with letters. Changing your password signs you out on your other devices. Forgot it? Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page: the link is sent to your email.

## Where you are signed in

The list shows each browser or device signed in to your account. If you do not recognise one, sign it out, or choose **Sign out all other devices**, and change your password.

## Two-step sign-in

Turn it on: see [Turn on two-step sign-in](help:two-step-sign-in). Store owners must have it.

## If something looks wrong

1. Change your password and sign out all other devices.
2. Check the [audit log](/settings/audit-log) for changes you did not make.
3. Tell Umar Techy from [Support](/support): see [Get help from Umar Techy](help:contact-umar-techy).

Never share your password or codes, also not with your team: give each person their own account instead.
