The store keeps what it needs to sell to you and deliver your orders: your name, contact details, addresses and orders. Its privacy policy, linked at the bottom of every page, says more.

## Messages you can stop

- **Marketing emails**: every one has an unsubscribe link.
- **WhatsApp order updates**: reply STOP.
- Emails about an order you placed (confirmation, delivery) are sent because you ordered.

## Your data

To see, correct or remove your data, [contact the store](store:/contact). Some records, such as invoices, are kept as long as the law requires.

## Stay safe

The store never asks for your password, your wallet PIN or your card number by email, phone or WhatsApp.
