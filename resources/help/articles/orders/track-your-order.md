## Emails we send

You get an email when your order is placed, when it is paid, when it is sent (with the tracking number when there is one) and when its delivery status changes.

## In your account

Signed in? Open [Your orders](store:/account/orders) and choose the order: you see its items, payment, delivery and expected date.

## Ordered without an account?

Your emails have the details of your order. To ask about it, [contact the store](store:/contact) with your order number.

## Cancel or change an order

Write to the store as soon as possible from the [contact page](store:/contact), or from your order with **Get help with this order**. Once an order has been sent, it can no longer be cancelled; you can ask for a return after it arrives.
