Open [Orders](/orders) and choose an order to see its items, customer, payments, shipments and timeline.

## How an order moves

You never set an order's status by hand. It moves as things happen:

1. **Placed**: the customer (or your team) places it. The stock is reserved and the customer gets a confirmation email.
2. **Paid**: the payment is recorded: online, or by you when cash or a transfer reaches you. See [Payments and refunds](help:payments-and-refunds).
3. **Shipped**: you create a shipment for the items. See [Ship your orders](help:ship-orders).
4. **Delivered**: the delivery is recorded, by the courier's tracking or by you.

Each step is in the order's **Timeline**, with who did it and when.

## Cancel an order

An order can be cancelled until it is being fulfilled: while it is placed, confirmed, processing or ready to ship. Choose **Cancel order** and give a reason. Reserved stock is released, and the customer is told by email.

## After delivery

Customers can ask for a return from their account (or, without an account, from the link sent to their email). See [Returns and refunds](help:returns-and-refunds).
