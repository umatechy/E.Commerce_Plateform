Open [Create order](/orders/new) (or **Create order** on the Orders page).

1. Choose the customer, or enter their details.
2. Add the products and quantities.
3. Add the delivery address and how it is delivered.
4. Create the order.

The server works out the prices, discounts, delivery and tax exactly as at checkout, and reserves the stock. The order then appears in [Orders](/orders) like any other, marked as created by your team.

## Good to know

- If your package has a monthly order limit and it is reached, new orders are refused until the next month or a plan change.
- A double click or a slow connection does not create the order twice.
