## Pages

[Pages](/content/pages) are your storefront's content pages, such as About us, Returns, Shipping and Privacy. A page is a **draft** until you publish it. Published pages appear in your storefront's footer and in your store's help center for shoppers.

Write your own policies: they are your promises to your customers. Umar Techy does not write them for you.

## SEO

[SEO](/content/seo) decides how your store appears in search engines and when links are shared: the title and description of your home page, and defaults for products and categories. A home page description is part of the setup checklist.

## Redirects

When an address changes (for example you rename a product), [Redirects](/content/redirects) send visitors of the old address to the new one, so links in old posts and search results keep working.
