Customers ask for a return from their account, or from the link sent to their email when they ordered without an account. Your team can also start one from the order. Open [Returns](/returns).

## The steps

1. **Approve** (or decline) the request. The customer is told by email and may send the items back. You can say how the items come back, who pays the return shipping, and add a message, such as where to send the parcel. Nothing is refunded yet.
2. **Sent back**: the customer, or you, adds the tracking number.
3. **Received**: the items have arrived. Stock does not change yet.
4. **Inspect**: say what became of every unit. Good units go back into stock; damaged ones are counted as damaged stock, which is never sold; units you do not accept go back to the customer. This cannot be changed afterwards.
5. **Approve the refund**: decide the amount: the accepted items, any shipping to refund, and a restocking fee only where your policy and the law allow one. You can give it as **store credit** instead of money.
6. **Refund**: someone who may issue refunds pays it back. The customer is told by email.

Instead of a refund you can **send the customer new items**: a replacement order is created and linked to the original.

Each step is shown on the return's timeline with who did it and when.
