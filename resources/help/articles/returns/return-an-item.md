Each store has its own return policy: read it on its pages (linked at the bottom of every page) before you ask.

## Ask for a return

- **With an account**: open the order under [Your orders](store:/account/orders) and ask for a return. Choose the items and say why. You can add photos.
- **Without an account**: open [Returns](store:/returns), enter your order number and the email you used. We send a link to that email: it works for 48 hours. Check your spam folder too.

## What happens next

1. The store approves your request (or explains why not) and tells you how to send the items back.
2. You send them and, if you have one, add the tracking number.
3. The store receives and checks the items.
4. The store refunds you, in the way you paid or as store credit, or sends you a new item. You are told by email.

You can follow every step on your return's page.
