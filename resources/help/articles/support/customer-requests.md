Customers write to you from your storefront's **Contact us** page or, when signed in, from their account. Each request appears in [Support](/support).

## Work through a request

1. Open it to read the conversation, the customer and any order it is about.
2. **Assign** it to someone in your team.
3. **Reply**. The customer gets your reply by email and sees it in their account, or through the private link we emailed them if they have no account.
4. Choose **Wait for the customer** when you need an answer from them, and **Mark resolved** when it is done.

You can add **internal notes** that the customer never sees.

A closed request cannot be reopened; the customer can open a new one if they need more help.

## Permissions

Reading requests, replying and managing them are separate permissions. Someone who can only read sees the requests but cannot reply.
