First search this help center: most questions are answered here. If you still need us, open [Support](/support) and choose **Help from the platform**, then **New request to the platform**.

## What to include

- What you were doing, what you expected and what happened instead.
- The page and, if you saw one, the error message.
- The **request ID**, if an error showed one. It lets us find exactly what happened.
- An order or invoice number, if it is about one.

## Never send us

- Your password, two-step codes or recovery codes.
- API keys, webhook secrets, or your payment provider passwords.

Umar Techy staff will never ask for these. If someone does, do not answer, and tell us.

## What happens next

Our replies appear in the request and are emailed to you. When the problem is solved, mark the request resolved and tell us how we did.

If our team needs to look at your store to help, every visit is recorded in the audit trail.
