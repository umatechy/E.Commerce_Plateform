Umar Techy does not decide your tax: **no rate is built in**. You enter the rates that apply to your business. If you are not sure which rates apply, ask your tax adviser.

Open [Tax](/settings/tax).

## 1. Tax classes

A class groups products taxed alike, for example Standard, Reduced or Zero rated. Products without a class use the **default class**. Choose a product's class on its page.

## 2. Rates

A rate belongs to a class and applies to a country (two letters, such as PK) and, if you want, one province or region (for example Punjab). Every rate that matches the address applies: a country rate and a region rate add up.

## 3. How tax is charged

- **Charge tax**: nothing is charged until this is on. It can be turned on once you have at least one active rate.
- **My prices include tax**: on, the price shoppers see already holds the tax, which the order shows separately; off, tax is added at checkout.
- **Tax is worked out from** the delivery address, the billing address, or your store's country (everyone pays the same).
- **Name shown to shoppers**, for example Tax, GST or Sales tax.
- Whether **delivery is taxed** (with the default class's rates), and how discounts and rounding are handled.

Use the **preview** to see the tax on an amount with your current rates, even before tax is turned on.

Changes apply to new orders. Placed orders keep the tax they were charged.
