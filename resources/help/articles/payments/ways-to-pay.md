Each store chooses which ways to pay it offers. You see them at checkout.

## Cash on delivery

Pay in cash when your order arrives. Some stores offer it only for orders within a certain amount.

## Bank transfer

Transfer the total to the store's account shown after you order. **Use your order number as the transfer reference.** The store sends your order once the money arrives.

## JazzCash or Easypaisa

Enter the mobile number of your wallet (and, if asked, the last 6 digits of your CNIC). You then get a request on your phone: approve it there. No card is needed.

If you did not approve in time or the payment failed, your order waits for payment. If you have an account, open it from [Your orders](store:/account/orders) to try again; otherwise [contact the store](store:/contact) with your order number.

## Safety

The store never asks for your wallet PIN or your password. Approve payments only on your own phone, and only for an order you placed.
