Open [Payments](/payments), or the **Payments** section of an order.

## Money you received yourself

For a **bank transfer**, check your bank first, then open the payment and record it as received. The order counts as paid and can be sent.

For **cash on delivery**, the courier collects the cash. When the courier pays you, open [Shipments](/shipments), choose **Cash to collect**, select the parcels the payment covers and record the amount with the courier's payment reference. Each order is then marked paid.

## Online payments

JazzCash and Easypaisa payments are recorded on their own when the customer approves them. If a customer did not complete it, the order shows as waiting for payment, and the customer can try again from the order.

## Refunds

Open the payment and choose **Refund**, then the amount (all or part). Online payments are refunded through the provider where it supports refunds; otherwise you pay the money back yourself and record it. The customer is told by email.

Refunds for returned items are made from the return. See [Returns and refunds](help:returns-and-refunds).

Who may record payments and give refunds depends on their role.
