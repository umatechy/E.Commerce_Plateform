Open [Payments setup](/settings/payments). Changes apply at checkout straight away; orders already placed keep their payment method. **Customers cannot place orders until at least one way to pay is on.**

## Cash on delivery

Customers pay the courier in cash when the order arrives. You record the cash when it reaches you. You can set a smallest and a largest order amount for cash on delivery: many stores limit cash orders to reduce returns.

## Bank transfer

Customers transfer the total to your bank account, using the order number as the reference. You confirm the payment once the money arrives, and then send the order.

Enter the account title (the name the bank shows), the bank, and the account number or IBAN. An IBAN works from every Pakistani bank and wallet. You can add short instructions. Because this decides where customers send money, you are asked to [confirm it is you](help:confirm-it-is-you).

## JazzCash and Easypaisa

Customers type their wallet number at checkout and approve the payment on their phone. The money goes to **your own merchant account** with the provider.

1. Get your merchant account details from JazzCash or Easypaisa.
2. Connect the account here with its **sandbox** (testing) details first. Customers never see a sandbox account.
3. Send a **test payment** of Rs 1.00 to check the details work.
4. Connect your **production** account to take real payments. This opens once Umar Techy has checked that provider.

Your details are saved encrypted and never shown again in full. Online payments must be included in your package.

## Cards

Visa and Mastercard payments will come through a card payment provider. Umar Techy is choosing one; it will appear here once it is ready.
