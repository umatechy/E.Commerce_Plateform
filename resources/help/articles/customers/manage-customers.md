[Customers](/customers) lists everyone who has an account with your store or was added by your team. Guests who never registered appear with their orders.

## A customer's page

Open a customer to see their orders, addresses, store credit, notes and support requests.

- **Notes** are for your team only.
- **Block** a customer to stop them signing in and ordering.
- **Store credit**: credit given, for example for a return, is used at checkout.

## Groups and tags

Under [Groups and tags](/customers/groups), each customer can be in one group and have as many tags as you need. Use them to find customers and to build campaign segments.

## Their data

Customers' details are personal data. Your team sees them only with permission, and exports are recorded in the [audit log](/settings/audit-log). Export only what you need, and keep files safe.
