Your **Dashboard** shows a setup checklist until every step is done. Each step has an **Open** link to the page where you complete it, and a **How?** link to the help for it. You can work through it over several visits: your progress is saved.

## Required before you can launch

- **A contact email for your store** in [Settings › Store details](/settings/general).
- **At least one product** that is active, public and has a price. See [Add a product](help:add-a-product).
- **A subscription** that is active or on trial.
- **Your first payment to Umar Techy**, when Umar Techy asks for payment before launch. Press **Get my first invoice**, pay it, and the team confirms it. See [Pay your invoices](help:billing-invoices).

The **Launch store** button stays unavailable until these are done.

## Optional, but customers notice

- **Categories**, so shoppers can browse.
- **A logo** in your published theme.
- **A way to pay**: cash on delivery or bank transfer. Without one, customers cannot place orders. See [Choose how customers pay](help:payment-methods).
- **An active shipping method**, so physical orders can be delivered. See [Set up delivery](help:shipping-setup).
- **A default warehouse**, so stock is shown and reserved.
- **Your policies** (returns, shipping, privacy) as published pages.
- **A test order**, to see the whole journey once.
- **A search engine description** for your home page.
- **A custom domain**, if your package includes one.

Steps your package does not include are not shown.

## Launching

Press **Launch store** on the dashboard. Before launch, visitors see "This store is getting ready and will open soon." After launch your shop is open to everyone at its address.

If you later want to stop selling, see [Close your store](help:close-store).
