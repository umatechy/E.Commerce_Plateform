Two-step sign-in protects your store even if someone learns your password. **Store owners must have it**: until it is on, the rest of the admin stays closed. Staff can turn it on too, and we recommend it.

## Turn it on

It takes about a minute.

1. Install an authenticator app on your phone, for example Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or Authy.
2. Open [Security](/security) from the menu under your name.
3. Enter your password and press **Turn on two-step sign-in**.
4. Scan the QR code with the app and enter the 6-digit code it shows.
5. Save the **recovery codes** you are given. You get 10. Keep them somewhere safe, away from your phone.

From now on, signing in asks for your password and then the current code from the app.

## If you lose your phone

Sign in with your password and type one of your recovery codes instead of the app's code. Each recovery code works once. Then turn on two-step sign-in again on your new phone.

To get new recovery codes, or to turn two-step sign-in off, go to [Security](/security) and enter your password and a current code.

If you have neither your phone nor a recovery code, contact Umar Techy support from [Support](/support). For your safety, support cannot turn it off on the strength of your name, email or an order number alone.

## Good to know

- After five wrong codes in a row, sign-in pauses for 15 minutes.
- Umar Techy staff will never ask you for your password, your codes or your recovery codes.
