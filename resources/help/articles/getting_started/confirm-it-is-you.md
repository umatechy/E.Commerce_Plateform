Some changes could cost you money or customers if someone else made them: for example where customers send bank transfers, your payment provider accounts, or closing the store. Before such a change, a **Confirm it is you** window asks for your password and, if two-step sign-in is on, a current code.

After you confirm, the change goes ahead. You are not asked again for about 15 minutes.

If you close the window, nothing is changed.

This protects you when a computer is left signed in or a session has been taken over. It is not a sign that something is wrong.
