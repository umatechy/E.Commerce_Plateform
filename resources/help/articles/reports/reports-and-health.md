## Dashboard

Your Dashboard shows sales for the period you choose, compared with the period before: revenue, orders, average order, new customers, payments collected and items low on stock. Each figure opens the page behind it.

## Reports

[Reports](/reports) breaks your figures down by sales, products, payments and more. You can export a report; the download link works for a limited time.

All amounts are in your store currency, and days follow your store's timezone. Figures are worked out by the server from your orders and payments; nothing is estimated.

Who can see revenue and reports depends on their role.

## Store health

[Store health](/store-health) checks your store's setup, subscription, package usage, domains and backups, and says what needs attention. Press **Check again** after you fix something. Earlier results are kept below.
