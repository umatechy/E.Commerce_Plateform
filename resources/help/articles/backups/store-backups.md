The platform backs up all data **every day**, and checks that backups can be read back.

## Take a backup yourself

On [Backups](/backups), take a backup before a big change, such as a large product import. You can start a few backups per hour. A backup that failed shows as **Failed**, and the platform team is told automatically.

Backup files cannot be downloaded from the admin. To take your data with you, use the CSV downloads on [Close store](/settings/close-store), which are available to the Owner at any time.

## Ask for a restore

Choose a backup and **Request a restore**. You are asked for your password. This only files a request: nothing is restored until Umar Techy staff review and authorise it. The request's status is shown on the page.

A restore brings back the store's data as it was at the time of the backup. Anything that happened after that backup is not in it, so take a fresh backup first if you are unsure.
