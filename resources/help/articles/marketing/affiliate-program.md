An affiliate program lets people you trust (influencers, bloggers, loyal customers) send shoppers to your store. They earn a commission on the orders they bring. You decide the rules and approve everyone. It is part of the Business and Premium packages.

## Set it up

On [Affiliates](/affiliates), open **Program settings**:

1. **Commission**: a percentage of the order, or a fixed amount per order. It is worked out on the products' price after discounts. Shipping and tax never count.
2. **Attribution window**: how many days after a visit through a link an order still counts (up to 90).
3. **When it is approved**: after delivery, after payment (for cash on delivery, once the cash is collected), and after a **hold period**. The hold period gives returns time to come in before you owe anything.
4. **An affiliate's own orders**: hold them for your review, or never pay them.
5. **Terms**: what affiliates agree to, for example where they may promote.

Save, then **Start program**. Each time you change the rules, a new version is saved. Commissions already earned keep the rules they were earned under.

## Add affiliates

- **Add affiliate** yourself, or turn on **Accept applications**. Your shop then shows a **Become an affiliate** page in its footer, and you approve or decline each application.
- Each affiliate gets a **referral code**. Their link is your shop's address followed by `?ref=` and the code, and it works on any page.
- You can also give them a **coupon code** (made under [Promotions](/promotions)). An order with that coupon counts for them even without the link. If an order has both, the coupon wins.
- When you add or approve someone, we email them a link to their **private page**. You can copy the link once, right then. If it is lost or shared, make a new one; the old one stops working.

## How orders are counted

- A visit through a link is remembered on that device for the attribution window. Visits are counted once per visitor a day.
- The commission is worked out once, when the order is placed. It never changes if you change the rules later.
- If an order looks like the affiliate's own (the same customer account, email or phone), it is held under **Commissions** for you to release or reject.
- Cancelled orders earn nothing. A refund takes back its share of the commission, even after you have paid it. That amount is taken from the next payout.

## Pay affiliates

You pay affiliates yourself, by bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa. Nothing is paid automatically.

1. Open the affiliate and choose **Prepare payout**. It gathers every approved commission and adjustment not paid yet.
2. You are shown where to send the money. This needs your password again, and every look is recorded in the [audit log](/settings/audit-log).
3. Send the money, then **Record as paid** with the transaction reference.

If you did not send it, **Cancel** the payout under **Payouts**, and its commissions become payable again. To correct a balance, add an **adjustment** with a reason. The affiliate sees it on their page.

## Who can do what

Managers and marketing staff can see the program and run it. Paying affiliates, changing their payout details and adjusting balances need the permission **Create and record affiliate payouts** (Administrators have it), and ask for your password again.
