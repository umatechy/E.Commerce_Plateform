## Messages your store sends

Your store emails customers on its own: order confirmations, payments, shipments, cancellations and refunds. [Messages](/notifications) shows what was sent, whether it arrived, and the wording. You can publish your own wording as a template; without one, the platform's wording is used.

## Order updates on WhatsApp and SMS

Customers can choose at checkout to get their order updates on WhatsApp. Updates are **never marketing**: only news about an order the customer placed. A customer can reply STOP to end them.

On [Messages](/notifications), open **WhatsApp & SMS**:

1. Connect your WhatsApp Business account or an SMS provider account (your own, from the provider). Details are saved encrypted.
2. Choose which updates go on which channel.
3. For WhatsApp, each update needs a **template that Meta approved**. Create it in WhatsApp Manager with the text shown, then enter its name and language here. Its approval is checked when you save, and again before use.
4. Set the **most messages a day**, to protect your balance if something goes wrong.

Email updates stay as they are.

## Email campaigns

1. Make a [segment](/segments): a group of customers, for example those who bought in the last 90 days.
2. Create a [campaign](/campaigns) for that segment, write it and send it.

Customers who unsubscribed are never sent campaigns. Every campaign email has an unsubscribe link.
