Open [Promotions](/promotions) and choose **Add promotion**.

## Discount

Choose the kind of discount:

- **Percentage**, for example 10% off.
- **Fixed amount**, for example Rs 200 off.
- **Free shipping**.

## Applies to

The whole order, or only some products, categories, brands or collections.

## Limits and dates

- **Needs a coupon code**: customers must type one of the promotion's codes at checkout. Without it, the discount applies on its own to every matching cart.
- **Minimum order value** and **largest discount**.
- **Total uses allowed** and **uses per customer**.
- **Start and end dates**.
- **Priority**: decides the order promotions are applied in.

Only **active** promotions apply at checkout.

## Coupon codes

Add codes under the promotion. Switch a code off to stop it without ending the promotion. To retire a whole promotion, set its status to **Archived**.

The discount is always worked out by the server at checkout, so a customer cannot change it.
