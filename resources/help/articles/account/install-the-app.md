Many shops can be added to your phone's home screen. The shop then opens like an app, full screen, with one tap.

## Android (Chrome, Edge, Samsung Internet)

When the shop offers it, tap **Install** in the small window that appears. Otherwise open the browser menu (⋮) and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

## iPhone and iPad (Safari)

Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

## Good to know

- Nothing is downloaded from an app store, and it takes almost no space.
- Without a connection you can look at pages you visited before. They may be out of date. To place an order you need a connection, so prices and stock are always correct.
- To remove it, hold the icon and choose **Remove** or **Uninstall**, as with any app.
- Signing out removes the pages kept from your visit.
