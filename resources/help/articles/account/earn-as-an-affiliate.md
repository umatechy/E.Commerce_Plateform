Some shops pay you a commission when people you send to them place an order. Look for **Become an affiliate** at the bottom of the shop.

## Joining

Apply with your name and email, and say where you will share your link. The shop reviews each application. If you are approved, we email you a link to your **private page**. Keep that link to yourself: anyone who has it can see your earnings.

## Your link

Your private page shows your own link and code. You may also have a coupon code for your followers. An order counts for you when:

- the shopper visited through your link, and ordered within the time the shop allows, **or**
- the shopper used your coupon code.

Your link is remembered on the shopper's own device. If they order on another device without using your link or coupon, the order cannot be counted.

## When you are paid

A commission is **pending** until the order has been delivered and paid, and the shop's return period has passed. It is then **approved**, and the shop pays you by bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa. Add your account on your private page.

If an order is cancelled, it earns nothing. If it is refunded, the refunded share is taken back, from your next payout if you were already paid. Orders you place for yourself may not count.

Your private page shows the date, value and commission of each order. It never shows who the customer was.
