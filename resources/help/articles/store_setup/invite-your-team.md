Open [Team](/team). You need permission to manage the team; the Owner always has it.

## Invite someone

1. Enter their email address and choose a **role**.
2. They receive an email with a link. The invitation is valid for 7 days; you can resend or withdraw it.
3. When they accept, they set their own password. You never see or choose it.

## Roles

A role decides which pages someone can open and what they can change. Your store comes with ready-made roles:

| Role | For |
|---|---|
| Owner | The store's owner: everything, including billing and closing the store |
| Administrator | Running the whole store day to day |
| Manager | Most of the store, without the most sensitive settings |
| Staff | Everyday work |
| Order Manager | Orders, payments and deliveries |
| Inventory Manager | Products and stock |
| Content & Marketing | Pages, SEO and marketing |

See exactly what each role may do under [Roles](/team/roles). You can also make your own roles there.

## Suspend or remove

Suspend a member to block their access for now, and reactivate them later. Remove them when they leave. Their past actions stay in the [audit log](/settings/audit-log).
