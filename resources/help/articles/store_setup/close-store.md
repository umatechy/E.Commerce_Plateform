Only the store's Owner can close it, from [Close store](/settings/close-store). You are asked for your password and two-step code.

## What closing means

1. **Your shop closes at once.** Customers see that it has closed and can no longer order.
2. **Billing stops.** No new invoices are issued; invoices already issued stay due.
3. **Your admin becomes read-only.** You can still see everything and download it, but not change it. Messages to customers and marketing stop.
4. **You can change your mind for 90 days.** Until then your data is kept and you can reopen the store exactly as it was.
5. **After that the store is archived.** It can no longer be reopened. Umar Techy may then delete its data for good; billing records are kept as the law requires.

Before you close, the page lists anything still open, such as unfinished orders, returns, store credit or unpaid invoices. Closing does not stop you, but your customers may be waiting on these.

## Take your data with you

Download your orders, customers, products and more as CSV files that open in Excel or Google Sheets. Do this before the store is archived.

## Reopen

While the store is closed and within the 90 days, open [Close store](/settings/close-store) and choose to reopen it. The shop opens to customers again, exactly as it was, and billing restarts from that day.
