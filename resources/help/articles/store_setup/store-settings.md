Open [Settings](/settings). Who can change settings depends on their role.

## Business information

Your store's contact email is required before you can launch.

## Currency

Your store sells in one currency: Pakistani rupees (Rs.) unless you chose another. Prices, orders and reports use it.

## Timezone

Dates in the admin and in reports follow your store's timezone, so "today" means today where you are.

## Languages

Your storefront can be shown in English and Urdu. Urdu pages read right to left. Choose which languages your storefront offers and which one is the default; shoppers can switch between them.

Product names and descriptions can be translated on each product. Order numbers, SKUs and prices are never translated.
