Open [Apps](/apps). The **Marketplace** tab lists apps reviewed by Umar Techy; **Installed** lists yours.

## Before you install

Open an app to read:

- **What it can see and do.** Apps can only read: they cannot change, delete, refund or pay anything. Permissions marked **Personal data** let it see your customers' names, addresses or phone numbers.
- **How it uses your data**, in the publisher's words, reviewed by Umar Techy.
- **The publisher**, their privacy policy and how to reach their support.

Some apps need a package that includes API access. The app's page says so if yours does not.

## Install and connect

Only the store owner can install apps.

1. Press **Install** and read the permissions again.
2. Tick that you allow them and press **Install and connect**. You are asked to [confirm it is you](help:confirm-it-is-you).
3. You go to the app's own site to finish connecting. When it is done, the app shows as **Active**.

If you left before it finished, open the app and choose **Continue connecting**.

## Settings, updates and usage

- **Settings**: some apps ask for details, such as an account name. Secret values (passwords, keys) are kept encrypted and never shown again; leave the field empty to keep the saved one.
- **Updates**: when a new version asks for more, you see exactly what is new and decide.
- **Last 30 days**: how often the app read your store and how many notifications it was sent, with failures.

## Pause or remove

- **Pause** stops the app at once; **Turn back on** lets it continue.
- **Remove app** ends its access at once and deletes your settings for it. Data it already copied to its own service is covered by its privacy policy; ask the publisher to delete it if you need to.

If Umar Techy finds a problem with an app, it can suspend it for every store at once. You then see why on the app's page.
