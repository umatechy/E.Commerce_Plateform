## Create a shipment

Open the order and choose **Create shipment**. Choose how it goes:

- **Own delivery**: your own rider.
- **Store pickup**: the customer collects it.
- **Another courier**: any courier you book yourself (for example by phone or in their portal). Add the tracking number now or later.

Choose the items in this parcel and the warehouse it leaves from. The cash to collect on delivery is worked out from the order's payment and printed on the label.

Booking a courier straight from the admin works only with couriers connected to the platform. Until one is connected for your store, use the options above.

## Follow your parcels

[Shipments](/shipments) lists every parcel. Use the views:

- **Needs attention**: parcels not yet booked or with a problem.
- **Cash to collect**: delivered cash-on-delivery parcels the courier still owes you for. When the courier pays, select them and record the amount: each order is then marked paid.
- **Return parcels**: items coming back from customers.

Update a shipment's status as it moves (for example delivered); the customer is told by email, and each update is shown in the tracking history.

## If the courier did not answer

If a booking got no answer from the courier, the parcel may or may not be booked. Look it up in the courier's portal before booking again: booking twice could send two riders.
