Stock is counted **per product (or variant) and per warehouse**. Open [Inventory](/inventory).

## Start tracking

1. Make sure you have a warehouse under [Warehouses](/warehouses). One of them is the **default**.
2. On Inventory, choose **Track stock for a product**, then the product and the warehouse.
3. **Set opening stock**: the quantity you have on hand now.

## The numbers

- **On hand**: what is physically there.
- **Reserved**: held for orders that are placed but not yet shipped.
- **Available**: on hand minus reserved. This is what can still be sold.
- **Damaged**: counted apart and never sold.

## Change stock

- **Adjust stock**: for example 5 to add five, -2 to remove two. Give a reason; it is kept in the movement history.
- **Mark stock as damaged**: the units leave the stock you can sell.
- **Write off damaged stock**: for damaged units thrown away or sent back to the supplier.

Every change is recorded under **Stock movements**, with who made it and why.

## Low stock

Set a **reorder point** on a stock record. At or under it, the item counts as **low on stock**, and your dashboard shows how many items are low.
